Privacy Policy for Kitchen Helper

Effective Date: October 19, 2026 • Application ID: com.amina.khelper

Key Summary: Kitchen Helper does not collect, transmit, share, or monetize any personal user data. All recipes, ingredient logs, and timers remain 100% offline on your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Kitchen Helper is committed to absolute user privacy. Our application:

  • Does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Does not collect advertising identifiers (AAID), device fingerprints, or IP addresses.
  • Does not track GPS or network location.
  • Does not access your contacts, camera, photos, microphone, or external storage.

2. Local Device Storage

All recipes you save, ingredients you mark, shopping list items, and timers are saved strictly in your device's local sandbox memory. This data is never synchronized with any remote server or third-party cloud infrastructure.

3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Kitchen Helper contains:

  • No Ads: No third-party banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertising SDKs.
  • No Trackers: No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Meta Pixel, or user telemetry SDKs.
  • No Data Sales: We do not sell or trade any user information to data brokers.

4. Permissions

The application requests zero sensitive Android system permissions. It operates fully within standard sandbox execution boundaries.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA)

Kitchen Helper is suitable for general audiences. Because we do not collect personal data of any kind, we strictly comply with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and international child privacy regulations.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • To delete all saved data, navigate to your device's Settings → Apps → Kitchen Helper → Storage → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all stored application data.
