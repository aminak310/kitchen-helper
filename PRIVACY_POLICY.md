# Privacy Policy for Kitchen Helper

**Effective Date:** October 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026

Kitchen Helper ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users ("you"). This Privacy Policy explains how Kitchen Helper handles information when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Kitchen Helper does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).**

Specifically:
- **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or account credentials.
- **No Device Identifiers:** We do not collect Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or IP addresses.
- **No Location Data:** We do not access, track, or collect GPS or network-based location information.
- **No Sensor or Media Access:** We do not request access to your device's camera, microphone, photo library, or contacts.

---

### 2. Local Device Storage

All information you generate within the app—including:
- Saved and favorite recipes
- Ingredient inventory lists
- Shopping list entries
- Kitchen timer configurations

is stored **locally on your device**. This information is never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud service.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services and Analytics

- **No Third-Party Advertising:** Kitchen Helper contains no third-party banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads.
- **No Analytics or Trackers:** We do not incorporate Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Meta SDKs, or any other tracking libraries.
- **No Data Selling:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with data brokers or commercial third parties.

---

### 4. Permissions

Kitchen Helper operates without requesting any sensitive Android system permissions. The application only accesses features strictly necessary to run locally on your device.

---

### 5. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Kitchen Helper complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations. Because our application does not collect any personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

---

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data remains on your physical device:
- You may clear your data at any time via your Android device's **Settings > Apps > Kitchen Helper > Storage > Clear Storage / Cache**.
- Uninstalling the application immediately and permanently removes all locally stored data.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
