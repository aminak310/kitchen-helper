Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kitchen Helper ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application (Package ID: com.amina.khelper).

Summary: Kitchen Helper is built with privacy as a priority. The app operates locally on your device, does not require an account, does not collect or transmit personal information, contains no third-party trackers or advertisements, and does not share data with anyone.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, device identifiers, or usage tracking data.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, address, or billing details. No account creation or login is required.
  • No Device & Hardware Identifiers: We do not collect Advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, IP addresses, or hardware fingerprints.
  • No Location Data: The app does not request, access, or track your GPS or network-derived location.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or external file storage.
  • No Third-Party SDK Data Collection: We do not integrate third-party analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) or advertising networks (such as AdMob or Meta Audience Network).

How We Use Information

Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

Any content you create or interact with in the app—such as your ingredient inventory, shopping lists, favorite recipes, and kitchen timers—is processed strictly locally on your device to enable the app's functionality:

  • Managing your pantry and ingredient stock.
  • Keeping track of items on your shopping list.
  • Displaying recipe instructions and cooking tips.
  • Running cooking timers with alerts on your device.

Sharing of Information

We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose your information to any third parties.

Because Kitchen Helper does not collect or transmit data to remote servers or third-party service providers, your data remains solely on your physical device. We do not transfer data to advertisers, data brokers, cloud storage providers, or business partners.

Data Security and Retention

All data generated through normal use of Kitchen Helper (such as ingredient checklists, shopping items, and timer preferences) is stored entirely in the local application sandbox on your device, secured by your mobile operating system's built-in access controls.

  • No Remote Storage: None of your data is stored on or transmitted to external servers.
  • Data Deletion: You have complete control over your data at all times. You can delete all local data by going to your device's Settings > Apps > Kitchen Helper > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling: Removing the app from your device automatically and permanently deletes all stored application data.

Children's Privacy

Kitchen Helper is a general-audience cooking utility and is safe for all users, including families. We comply fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy frameworks. Because our application does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if our application features or applicable legal obligations change. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
